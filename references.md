# Initial Reference Shelf

This is a starting bibliography for comparative method and selected historical contexts. It is not a substitute for community review, primary sources, or tradition-specific scholarship. The descriptions indicate scope, not endorsement of every classification used by a source.

1. OpenStax, *Introduction to Sociology 2e*, “15.2 World Religions”  
   https://openstax.org/books/introduction-sociology-2e/pages/15-2-world-religions  
   An accessible introduction to broad comparative categories. Use such categories cautiously: practitioner understandings may not align neatly with external labels.

2. Fabio Rambelli, “Buddhism and Shinto,” *Oxford Research Encyclopedia of Religion* (2018)  
   https://doi.org/10.1093/acrefore/9780199340378.013.612  
   A scholarly overview of the historically complex interaction of Buddhist institutions, kami traditions, and local cults in Japan.

3. American Anthropological Association, *Perspectives: An Open Introduction to Cultural Anthropology*, chapter “Religion”  
   https://perspectives.americananthro.org/Chapters/Religion.pdf  
   An introductory anthropological discussion of religious concepts and variation. Use as methodological orientation, not a replacement for community authority.

## Citation practice

- Cite the exact edition, translation, page, archive, or URL used for each claim.
- Distinguish primary sources, practitioner accounts, community-authored sources, and scholarly interpretation.
- Do not cite a general survey as proof of a specific local practice.
- Where a tradition's own sources or practitioners disagree with a secondary classification, preserve the disagreement and explain the source context.
- Add references only after checking that the source supports the statement attached to it.
