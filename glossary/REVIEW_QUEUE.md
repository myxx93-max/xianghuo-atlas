## Review policy

- Do not mark an entry COMMUNITY_REVIEWED without a real review by an appropriately situated contributor.
- A reviewer may request attribution, anonymity, restricted access, or withdrawal.
- Conflicting definitions should be retained with attribution rather than averaged into one description.
- Entries may be split when a single term has distinct meanings across communities, texts, or periods.
