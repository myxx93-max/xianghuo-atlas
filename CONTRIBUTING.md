# Contributing to Xianghuo Atlas

Thank you for helping improve this project. Contributions may be written in English or another language. Preserve original-language names and explain translation choices.

## Before opening a contribution

1. Read the [project principles](README.md) and [consent guide](contribution-guide.md).
2. Choose the smallest useful contribution: a terminology correction, source citation, community note, translation, or documented disagreement.
3. Use the templates in [templates/](templates/).
4. Do not upload private, restricted, initiatory, or sensitive community knowledge.
5. Separate what a source says from your interpretation.

## Contribution types

- **Correction:** identify the exact statement, explain the issue, and provide a source or community context.
- **Tradition profile:** describe a specific community or historical context without claiming to represent everyone in it.
- **Source record:** document provenance, date, language, edition, access, and rights.
- **Translation note:** preserve the original term, transliteration, literal gloss, contextual meaning, and limits of translation.
- **Comparison proposal:** state the precise dimension being compared and why the relationship is not necessarily equivalence.
- **Restriction or withdrawal request:** ask maintainers to remove, redact, or restrict material that should not be public.

## Review workflow

1. Maintainers check scope, provenance, rights, and safety.
2. A relevant community or subject-matter review is sought where feasible.
3. Unresolved disagreements remain visible and attributed.
4. Records are marked with a review status; merging does not mean theological endorsement.
5. Corrections and withdrawal requests are handled promptly.

## Suggested pull request description

- What is being added or corrected?
- Which community, source, language, region, and period does it concern?
- Which statements are directly sourced and which are interpretation?
- What permissions or license apply?
- Has a relevant community reviewed it?
- Are any details restricted or sensitive?

A contribution may be declined or kept out of the public repository if permission, provenance, or community authority is unclear. Completeness is never more important than care.
