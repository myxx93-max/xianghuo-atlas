# The Heart of Xianghuo Atlas
# 香火图谱的中心

> **怀念来处，传承记忆，寻找同根。**  
> **Remembering Our Origins. Preserving Our Inheritance. Searching for Our Shared Roots.**

## Our central purpose

Humanity has never lived without memory.

Across mountains, rivers, oceans, and generations, people have remembered their ancestors, honored sacred beings, told stories of creation, celebrated the cycles of nature, and searched for meaning beyond their immediate lives.

They gave these memories different names, languages, symbols, and forms.

Some became gods and goddesses. Some became ancestors, spirits, kami, devas, saints, Buddhas, or sacred presences. Others were preserved in landscapes, temples, scriptures, oral histories, and rituals.

Many of these memories remain alive. Others are fragmented, misunderstood, or disappearing.

**Xianghuo Atlas begins with a simple belief: these memories deserve to be remembered, understood, and passed on.**

Our purpose is not to build a universal pantheon. It is to create an open knowledge space in which different peoples can preserve their own cultural memories, explain their own traditions, and explore possible connections with other civilizations without surrendering their distinct identities.

## 1. Remembering — Where We Come From

We seek to recover forgotten names, stories, traditions, and cultural memories.

Not merely to catalogue sacred figures, but to understand the people, places, experiences, hopes, and fears that gave them meaning.

We remember those who came before us, including voices that history did not preserve.

## 2. Inheriting — What We Carry Forward

Every culture has something worth preserving.

We invite people from different traditions to document their heritage in their own languages and according to their own understanding.

We do not seek to replace their identities with a universal system. We seek to give their memories a place where they can survive, grow, and be passed to future generations.

**Inheritance is not uniformity. It is continuity through difference.**

## 3. Searching for Shared Roots — What May Connect Us

Across distant civilizations, we encounter recurring stories, symbols, sacred figures, rituals, and ideas.

Are these similarities born from shared human experiences? Ancient migrations? Cultural exchanges? Lost historical connections? Or independent discoveries made by different peoples?

We do not begin with predetermined answers.

We investigate, compare, question, and preserve evidence.

We distinguish historical connections from resemblance, hypotheses from established facts, and shared human experiences from claims of common ancestry.

**Searching for shared roots does not mean demanding a single origin. It means refusing to abandon the questions that may connect us.**

## 4. A Place for Every Voice

This atlas belongs neither to one religion nor to one civilization.

It welcomes practitioners, historians, linguists, archaeologists, storytellers, researchers, and anyone who wishes to understand and preserve cultural memory.

Every tradition has the right to describe itself.

Every community has the right to preserve its boundaries.

Every contributor has the right to question our assumptions.

No culture needs to surrender its uniqueness to participate.

## Our commitments

- We remember without appropriating.
- We compare without forcing equivalence.
- We search without inventing answers.
- We preserve without claiming ownership of another people's heritage.
- We pass forward what we can, while respecting what should remain private.

Perhaps we will discover connections that history has obscured. Perhaps some questions will remain unanswered. Both are worth preserving.

Because the purpose is not simply to discover where the gods came from.

It is to understand how humanity remembers, how civilizations inherit, and how distant peoples may be connected.

**We remember our past. We preserve our inheritance. We search for the roots that may connect us all.**

## 中文中心宣言

### 怀念：寻找正在消失的记忆

人类曾经通过神话、祭祀、祖先、神庙、口述故事和节日，记录自己对世界的理解。

我们希望保存的不只是神明的名字，更是名字背后的人、土地、故事、情感和生活。

- 怀念已经远去的祖先与文明。
- 保存逐渐消失的地方信仰和文化记忆。
- 重新发现被历史、迁徙和语言隔开的故事。
- 让后代知道，他们从哪里来。

### 传承：让不同文明继续讲述自己

传承不是把过去封存在博物馆里，而是让记忆继续生长。

每个文化都可以用自己的语言、自己的分类和自己的理解讲述自己。

**传承不是统一，而是让差异不再因为遗忘而消失。**

### 寻找同根：追问人类文明之间的联系

不同地区的神话、洪水传说、创世叙事、祖先记忆、自然崇拜和英雄故事，为什么会出现某些相似的表达？

它们究竟来自共同的生存经验、相似的自然环境、古代迁徙与文化交流、更早期的故事传播，还是彼此独立产生的相似观念？

我们不预设答案。

我们寻找线索，保存证据，记录差异，也允许最终找不到共同起源。

**寻找同根，不是证明所有文明都来自同一个源头，而是不放弃追问彼此之间可能存在的联系。**

## 我们的四条边界

- 我们寻找同根，但不要求同源。
- 我们尊重差异，但不拒绝比较。
- 我们怀念过去，但不把过去神秘化。
- 我们传承文化，但不占有文化。

## 项目定位

香火图谱是一个以人类文化记忆为基础，以神圣传统为入口，以跨文明关系探索为长期方向的开放知识工程。

过去：寻找被遗忘的来处。  
现在：保存仍然活着的文化。  
未来：让下一代能够继续理解和传承。

「香火」是我们的文化起点：有人离去，有人记得，有人继续守护，也有人重新点燃。

它不必成为所有文化共同接受的宗教概念，却可以成为我们发起这项工作的象征。

**我们不是要创造一个共同的神系，而是希望有一天，不同土地上的人都能在这里找到自己的记忆，也发现自己与其他文明之间值得探索的联系。**
