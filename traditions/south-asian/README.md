# South Asian Traditions

**Collection status: OPEN FOR COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION — NOT YET REVIEWED**

“South Asian traditions” is a geographic working label, not one religion or unified system. Contributions may concern specific Hindu sampradayas, regional devotional traditions, Buddhist, Jain, Sikh, and other communities, languages, texts, and historical contexts.

Preserve terms in their original languages and scripts. Explain the specific community's use of words such as deva, devi, Ishvara, Brahman, avatar, murti, bhakti, Tirthankara, Buddha, bodhisattva, Guru, and Ik Onkar where relevant. Do not assume these terms are interchangeable or that one school speaks for all.

## Priority contribution needs

- community-specific terminology and translation notes;
- school, lineage, region, language, and period identification;
- primary text editions and reliable scholarship;
- accounts of internal diversity and contested interpretations;
- guidance on images, names, and practices that should not be reproduced.

Use the [tradition profile template](../../templates/tradition-profile.md). This page is a contribution scaffold, not a complete introduction.
