# Tradition Collections

These folders are **working entry points**, not a definitive taxonomy of humanity's religions or cultures. They are grouped for navigation only. Traditions cross borders, overlap, change over time, and may reject the labels used here.

Each collection should be built with contributors who understand the relevant language and community context. A folder's existence does not imply that its contents are complete or community-approved.

## Adding a collection

Use the [tradition profile template](../templates/tradition-profile.md). Prefer a specific community, lineage, region, language, or historical corpus over an overly broad label. If the community has its own preferred structure, follow it rather than this folder scheme.

Do not add restricted knowledge, sensitive site coordinates, or material without appropriate permission.
