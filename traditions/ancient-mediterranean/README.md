# Ancient Mediterranean Contexts

**Collection status: OPEN FOR CONTRIBUTION — HISTORICAL SCOPE MUST BE SPECIFIED**

This working collection may include ancient Greek, Roman, and neighboring Mediterranean religious contexts. Avoid treating “Greek” and “Roman” as timeless, uniform categories or reducing Roman practice to renamed Greek figures.

Distinguish literary narrative, inscriptions, archaeology, civic and household practice, local cults, philosophical writing, later reception, and modern reconstruction. Where figures are compared across languages or regions, record the historical source and the nature of the comparison rather than assuming identity.

## Priority contribution needs

- precise date, place, language, and source corpus;
- primary text and inscription references;
- archaeological catalog identifiers where applicable;
- translation history and interpretive disagreement;
- distinction between ancient evidence and modern reception.

Use the [tradition profile template](../../templates/tradition-profile.md). This folder is a historical research entry point, not a universal classical pantheon.
