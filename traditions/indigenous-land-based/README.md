# Indigenous and Land-Based Traditions

**Collection status: COMMUNITY-LED ONLY — NO GENERALIZED CATALOGUING**

There is no single Indigenous religion or universal category for land-based traditions. Communities may have their own protocols governing who can speak, what can be recorded, when knowledge can be shared, and whether a place, name, story, image, or practice may be made public.

This collection must not be populated by outsiders simply because information is discoverable. Community authority, consent, data sovereignty, and the right not to disclose take precedence over research completeness.

## Before any contribution

- identify the specific community using its preferred public name;
- establish who has authority to authorize the material;
- ask whether documentation and public hosting are appropriate;
- follow community protocols for attribution, access, storage, and withdrawal;
- avoid precise locations or details that could expose vulnerable places or people.

A community may choose to create a page explaining that certain knowledge is not available for public documentation. That boundary is itself respected knowledge. Do not use the general profile template if it conflicts with community protocols.
