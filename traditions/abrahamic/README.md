# Abrahamic Traditions

**Collection status: OPEN FOR CONTRIBUTION — TRADITION-SPECIFIC REVIEW REQUIRED**

This working collection may include distinct Jewish, Christian, Muslim, and related historical or community contexts. It must not imply that these traditions share one theology, vocabulary, canon, or understanding of sacred figures.

Do not model the one God as one member of a pantheon. Record tradition-specific language, scriptural and interpretive sources, liturgy, prayer, angels, prophets, saints where recognized, sacred places, and community practice with careful attribution. Respect restrictions around divine names, images, texts, and representation.

## Priority contribution needs

- tradition- and school-specific terminology;
- precise scripture edition, translation, chapter, and verse;
- interpretive tradition and historical context;
- practitioner review and corrections;
- clear handling of contested or sensitive material.

Use the [tradition profile template](../../templates/tradition-profile.md). This folder is not a comparative theology conclusion.
