# Japanese Traditions

**Collection status: OPEN FOR COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION — NOT YET REVIEWED**

This working collection may cover specific shrine traditions, kami-related practices, Buddhist schools and institutions, local cults, ancestor practices, historical texts, and modern communities. It must not project one uniform system across all periods.

Preserve Japanese terms, kanji/kana forms, readings, regional usage, and the source context. Do not translate every kami as “god”. Historical relations among shrine traditions, Buddhism, local practice, and institutions require period-specific evidence.

## Priority contribution needs

- community and shrine-specific terminology;
- historical distinctions and documented interactions;
- source references with edition, date, and passage;
- careful translation notes;
- modern practitioner perspectives clearly distinguished from historical evidence.

Use the [tradition profile template](../../templates/tradition-profile.md). No claim of completeness or community endorsement is made.
