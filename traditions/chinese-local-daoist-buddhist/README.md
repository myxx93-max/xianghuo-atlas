# Chinese Local, Daoist, and Buddhist Contexts

**Collection status: OPEN FOR COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION — NOT YET REVIEWED**

This working collection is deliberately not titled “Chinese pantheon”. It may include distinct local religious communities, Daoist lineages and ritual traditions, Buddhist schools and institutions, household and ancestor practices, regional temple networks, and historical interactions among them.

Do not assume that every temple, deity, ritual, or local practice belongs to one unified system. Preserve regional names, scripts, pronunciations, lineages, source periods, and local explanations.

## Priority contribution needs

- regional and local terminology with source context;
- distinctions among community, lineage, institution, and historical period;
- public inscriptions, gazetteers, temple records, and scholarship with precise citations;
- practitioner explanations where contributors have permission to share them;
- corrections to overgeneralized “Chinese religion” descriptions.

## Boundaries

Do not publish private ritual manuals, restricted lineage materials, personal data, or sensitive shrine locations without explicit authorization.

Use the [tradition profile template](../../templates/tradition-profile.md). This page is an entry point, not a factual survey or an endorsement of any single interpretation.
